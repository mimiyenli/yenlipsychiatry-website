FAQ

Honest answers to real questions.

The things people most often want to know before scheduling a first session.

Do you take insurance?

I'm out-of-network with most plans, which lets me keep appointments long and unhurried. I provide superbills you can submit for partial reimbursement.

How long does a session last?

Initial consults are 60 minutes; ongoing sessions are 50. I don't do 15-minute med checks.

Do you only prescribe medication?

No. Medication is one tool among several. Many sessions look more like therapy than a pharmacy visit.

Is everything telehealth?

Yes — secure video sessions across California, including evenings.

What's the first session like?

An hour-long conversation about what's going on, what you've already tried, and what you're hoping for.

How do I know if you're the right fit?

I offer a free 15-minute call before booking. We'll talk about what you're looking for.

Still have questions?

The shortest path is a quick call. No pressure, no commitment — just a conversation.
